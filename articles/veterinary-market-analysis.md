# Veterinary Market: USD 172.76 Billion Forecast by 2030

The global veterinary market is projected to reach USD 172.76 billion by 2030, expanding at a CAGR of 6.5% from 2025. This growth is primarily fueled by a significant increase in pet adoption rates and the rising prevalence of parasitic diseases in livestock and companion animals.

## Market Dynamics and Growth Drivers

The veterinary industry is undergoing rapid expansion due to the dual influence of increasing pet ownership and heightened awareness of animal health. In 2023 alone, approximately 4.8 million animals were adopted into homes, creating a sustained demand for diagnostic and therapeutic services. ## Regional Market Performance

### North America Dominance
North America currently maintains the largest market share, supported by a robust infrastructure of advanced clinics and a high concentration of professionals. The United States leads the world in veterinary employment, with over 127,000 active veterinarians. Technological innovation, such as the launch of AI-powered diagnostic tools like the Vetscan Opticell, continues to solidify this region's leadership position.

### Asia-Pacific Growth Trends
The Asia-Pacific region is recognized as the fastest-growing market, driven largely by the industrialization of poultry and livestock farming. In China, per capita poultry consumption has increased nearly tenfold over three decades, necessitating greater investment in feed additives and vaccines. Government initiatives in India, such as the National Livestock Mission, are also playing a critical role in controlling infectious diseases like Foot & Mouth disease through mass vaccination programs.
